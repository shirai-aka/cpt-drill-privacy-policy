CPT合格ドリル (CPT Pass Drill)

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

This English page is a reference translation of the Japanese original, provided for convenience. The Japanese text is the official version; where the two differ, the Japanese text applies.

CPT合格ドリル (CPT Pass Drill; the “App”) is a question-bank app organized by topic, intended for preparing for personal trainer certification exams. The App can be used without membership registration, and study records are stored only on the device.

1. Information Collected

1.1 Information collected by the App itself

The App does not collect personal information such as names or email addresses. No account registration is required either.

Study records (answer status, accuracy rate, question history, in-app progress state, etc.) are stored only on the user’s device and are never transmitted to, stored on or collected by the developer’s or any third party’s server.

1.2 Information collected through ad delivery

The App uses Google AdMob (Google LLC) to display ads. AdMob may collect the following information for ad delivery and measurement.

  • Identifiers (advertising identifier IDFA, device ID)
  • Usage data (information about interactions with ads, such as views and taps)
  • Coarse location (a rough location derived from the IP address; location permission is not requested)
  • Diagnostic data (information about crashes and performance inside the ad SDK)

Tracking (use of the advertising identifier across other companies’ apps and websites) takes place only if you consent to iOS tracking permission (ATT).

For details, please see Google’s policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google’s explanation of advertising: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

2. Tracking Permission (iOS)

In accordance with the iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework, the App shows a dialog asking for permission to track.

  • If you choose “Allow”: personalized ads based on your interests may be shown
  • If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”: non-personalized ads are shown

Whichever you choose, you can use all of the App’s study features in the same way. The setting can be changed at any time from iOS “Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking”.

3. Purposes of Use

Information collected in connection with ad delivery is used only for the purposes of delivering, displaying and measuring the effectiveness of ads. The developer does not use it for the purpose of identifying individuals.

4. Provision to Third Parties

Except where required by law, the developer does not provide user information to third parties. The handling of information in connection with ad delivery is governed by the Google policies above.

5. Device Permissions

The App does not request permissions such as location, contacts, photos or camera.
(If a new permission becomes necessary in a future version, this policy will be updated and the change announced on this page.)

6. Data Management and Deletion

If you uninstall the App, the study records stored on the device are deleted.
