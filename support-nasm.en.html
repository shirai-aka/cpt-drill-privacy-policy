FAQ

Q. How many questions are there?

8 topics and 640 questions in total, all available free of charge. All questions are original creations.

Q. What is the “集中ゲージ” (Focus Gauge)?

It is a gauge that is consumed by 1 each time a drill session (10 questions) is started (maximum 5). It automatically recovers by 1 every 30 minutes, and can be fully restored immediately by watching a video ad. Review sessions of questions you got wrong do not consume the gauge.

Q. What is the mock exam?

It is a timed exam format that draws questions from all topics in a distribution close to that of the real exam. You can choose a mini mock exam (30 questions, 36 minutes) or the full format (100 questions, 120 minutes, matching the Japanese-language real exam). Right and wrong answers are not shown while you are answering; after finishing, you can check your accuracy rate by topic and the explanation for each question. After confirmation, a video ad plays before the exam starts (if the ad cannot be loaded, the exam starts anyway). Mock exams do not consume the Focus Gauge. The pass line on the results screen is a guide for study purposes and does not guarantee passing or failing the actual exam.

Q. About ads

This app is a free app supported by advertising revenue. In addition to the banner at the bottom of the screen, a full-screen ad is shown between study sessions and a video ad before a mock exam starts. Even if you set tracking to “Ask App Not to Track”, non-personalized ads are shown.

Q. Where is my study data stored?

All of it is stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to external servers. If you delete the app, the data is deleted as well. Transferring data when switching devices is not supported.

Q. Can I use it offline?

Questions, explanations and results records can be used offline (except for displaying ads).
